Puzzle stylesBloom Gather+

Tap 3+ same-colour gems touching up, down, left or right. Pairs do not clear. No swapping or diagonals. Groups can turn corners along shared edges. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesCross Gather+

Tap a gem: clear 2+ of its colour in straight lines up/down/left/right. No turns. The tapped gem counts toward the total. Each line stops at a different colour, gap or blocker. Lines cannot turn corners or go diagonally. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesDiagonal Gather+

Tap 2+ same-colour gems touching by an edge OR a corner. No swapping. The whole connected group clears, including diagonal connections. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesDiagonal Match+

Swap neighbours, including diagonally. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe between neighbouring gems, or tap one and then the other. You may swap diagonally, but a diagonal line does not count as a match.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesGather+

Tap 2+ same-colour gems touching up, down, left or right. No swapping or diagonals. Tap any gem in the connected group to clear it. Groups can turn corners along shared edges. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Puzzle stylesMatch 3+

Swap up, down, left or right. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe between neighbouring gems, or tap one and then the other. Diagonal swaps and diagonal matches do not count. A swap that makes no match moves back.

Puzzle stylesRising Tides+

Swap to match 3+ in a row or column. Gems rise; deliver relics to the TOP arrows. Swap neighbouring gems up, down, left or right. Gems refill from below. Clear ABOVE a relic to lift it to the top exit.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesRiver Rows+

Shift a row one space left or right. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe left or right, or tap two horizontal neighbours. The connected row wraps at its ends; gaps and blockers stop the segment. Tap a power to fire it, or shift its row to fire it. A shift with no match or power moves back.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesStar Hop+

Swap in an L: two squares, then one sideways. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Tap a gem, then its destination, or drag between them. The destination is two squares across and one up/down, or two up/down and one across. Only the two endpoint gems swap; the gems between them stay put.

Puzzle stylesWind Columns+

Shift a column one space up or down. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe up or down, or tap two vertical neighbours. The connected column wraps at its ends; gaps and blockers stop the segment. Tap a power to fire it, or shift its column to fire it. A shift with no match or power moves back.

Puzzle rulesMake A Bomb+

Join a row of 3 and a column of 3 at one shared gem: 5 gems forming a T or L. This creates a bomb. In Gather styles, clear a group of 7 or 8 to create one. Swap or match a bomb to explode. In Gather and shifting styles, tap it to explode.

Puzzle rulesRainbow Power+

In swap and shifting styles, match five in a straight line. In Gather styles, clear a group of 9 or more. Swap the rainbow gem with a color to clear that color. In Gather or shifting styles, tapping it clears its own gem color.

Puzzle rulesRow & Column Powers+

In swap and shifting styles, match four in a row to make a power gem. In Gather styles, clear a group of 5 or 6 to make a row power. Swap it with a neighbor to clear its row or column. In Gather or line-shifting styles, tap it; shifting a powered line also fires it.

Puzzle rulesSet Off Powers+

Create powers with 4 in a line, 5 in a line, or 5 in a T or L. Gather styles instead use groups of 5–6 for row powers, 7–8 for bombs, or 9+ for rainbows. Then swap or match them to fire; in Gather or shifting styles, tap a power. Shifting its line also activates it. Making one alone does not count as firing it.

Worlds & questsBuild Your World+

Tap a highlighted building on My Map. Its dock shows the saved stages and cost; construction happens at that same parcel. Close the dock to keep exploring. Win 1, 2 or 3 Build Coins to match your level rating. Improving a previous rating earns only the difference; repeating it earns none. Build stages cost 4, 6 and 10 Build Coins: 20 per project, 80 per world. Gold coins are separate. After five first wins in a chapter, the keeper supplies only the coins still missing to finish eligible projects, counting your saved balance first. This help is awarded once per chapter. Saved credits are used before new support, so surplus gradually pays for future construction. Existing buildings stay paid and saved balances are preserved.

Bonus gamesBonus Scatters+

Count Bonus symbols anywhere in the 15 full cells. Exactly two return your bet once; three unlock 2 free spins at 1× coins; four unlock 3 at 2×; five or more unlock 6 at 3×. Every 3+ trigger also opens Pip’s Firefly Flight. Free spins can retrigger both rewards. Flight winnings do not use the free-spin multiplier. See FIREFLY FLIGHT below for controls, health and earned prizes. Older unfinished lantern and chest bonuses keep their original rules.

Bonus gamesDaily Discovery+

Open Discovery beside Wheel and Slots. Compare both pictures and tap a changed detail in either one. Pinch to zoom and drag to inspect. Find eight changes before your misses run out. Your first hint in each round is free; later hints cost 50 coins. A pink ring marks the detail; viewing an existing hint again is free. The play count includes this realm’s free or unfinished scene plus saved Discovery tokens. The first three completed scenes across all realms each award 100 coins and one hint daily; further scenes are practice. Prizes reset at local midnight. New rounds use visible object changes: a missing piece, a different shape or a changed count. Subtle color or shading changes are not clues. The three most recent compositions are avoided. The previous five clue sets are avoided. Unfinished rounds keep your clues, hints and progress, including after midnight. A Discovery replay token starts a fresh round after a finished or failed scene with one free hint and an extra completion reward; use the token replay button on the Discovery result panel, or open Inventory → Tokens.

Bonus gamesDaily Pairs+

Open Pairs beside Wheel, Slots and Discovery, or in Menu → Bonus. Find ten matching pairs among 20 cards. New rounds rotate through all 50 friends; saved rounds keep their faces. Revealing two cards counts as one move. Finish in 10–12 moves for one rainbow booster, 13–20 for one hammer, 21–30 for 150 coins, or 31+ for 75 coins. Unfinished six-pair rounds keep their original reward thresholds. No entry fee, timer or life cost. Cards flip as you reveal them; matching pairs sparkle. Mismatches stay visible briefly. Cards and moves save when you leave. One completed round per local day; unfinished rounds resume after midnight and use the completion day's allowance. Rewards save automatically; the last pair celebrates before the prize panel opens. Close the panel to return. Badges count the free or unfinished round plus saved tokens. Extra rounds: buy a Pairs token in Coin Shop → Tokens or Market Place → Token Packs and use it directly in Pairs or Inventory → Tokens after completing today’s round.

Bonus gamesFirefly Flight+

Guide Pip with touch, drag, or arrow keys. Rescue five fireflies in each clearing, then enter its portal. Each clearing gets harder: 2 gusts, then 3 faster gusts and 2 thorn patches, then 4 fast crossing gusts and 4 thorn patches. Steer around the purple thorns and moving gusts. A one-second safe entry lets you orient yourself in each clearing. Start with 100% bonus health. Each gust or thorn hit removes 25%; a brief recovery shield prevents repeated damage from the same contact. At 0%, the flight ends and you keep only what you earned. Each rescue earns 1/15 of the coin treasure, rounded down cumulatively. Any available non-rainbow booster unlocks at 10 rescues; an available rainbow unlocks at 15. The bottom panel shows earned winnings, starting at zero. Health and rescues are saved; reopening does not heal Pip. Bonus health is separate from puzzle lives. Your regular free spins wait for your return.

Bonus gamesTreasure Slots+

Use −/+ to select 50–1,000 coins in steps of 25. Tap PRESET BETS to choose 50, 100, 250, 500 or 1,000 coins. MAX BET selects your highest affordable stake up to 1,000; choosing a stake does not spin. Hints can start at three matches; other boosters need four, and rainbows need five. Coins scale with your bet; boosters do not.
