Practise a move with Sunflower Valley’s flower gems. Choose a puzzle style, then tap the board or listen to the game’s guide.

Character boards begin at level 16. After the opening ten levels, one puzzle in each group of ten uses faces from the cast. Match identical faces using the level’s usual rules. Replaying that level brings back the same cast.

Hints & Tips

Plan a move, clear an obstacle, or turn a match into a bomb.

Match what the goal asks for

Need blue gems? Look for a blue match before spending a move on another colour. Tap the goal panel during a level to check how many you still need.

Match on ice, beside a crate

A match on an icy tile removes one layer. To open a crate, match gems next to it. The dots on each obstacle tell you how many hits it still needs.

Make a bomb, then fire it

In a swap puzzle, match five gems in a T or L. In Gather, tap a connected group of seven or eight. If your goal counts activations, fire the bomb to earn credit.

From the map to the worksite

Pick a realm

Open My Story and choose a route. Two cards show gold fares, one asks for one rewarded ad and one asks for two. Preview the choice and confirm the displayed cost. The destination becomes the puzzle realm for the next five levels. A saved route can be revisited without buying it again.

Decide how the encounter ends

Listen to the encounter and choose one of its two endings. The choice determines Pip’s keepsake and can change the gold outcome and the destinations offered next. Chapter notes remember what happened.

Leave your mark on the landmark

Tap the project on its map parcel. Use your chapter keepsake to preview four stage designs with the displayed gold or ad costs. Save your choice, then spend Build Coins to assemble the stage. Watch the detail appear on the landmark. At a finished project, Decorate with can add a missing detail.

Building a landmark

Your first win earns one Build Coin per star. Winning a replay earns gold. A higher best rating also earns one Build Coin per extra star. Going from one star to three adds two Build Coins. Repeating a three-star win adds none.

Getting startedGame Updates+

When an update is required, tap Google Play on the update screen, install the update, then return to Pip’s Adventure. If the update is still required, check your internet connection and try Google Play again. Updating the installed game keeps its saved progress. Contact support before uninstalling or clearing app data.

Getting startedChoose Your Progress+

When a different Google Play save is found, compare the level and coin balance shown for each option. Use Google Play Save replaces progress on this device and keeps a local recovery copy. Keep Current Progress preserves this device’s progress and enables its automatic backups. The two saves are not merged. Choose one option to continue.

Link to this tip ↗
Getting startedSave Status+

Open Menu → Settings → Save Status. Progress saves on this device automatically. On Android, Google Play Games also backs up when your profile and the service are available; no separate game account is needed. Use the same Play Games profile on a replacement Android device. Check the status before switching devices. Backups can be delayed while offline or when the service is unavailable. Apple/iCloud saving and Android-to-iPhone transfer are not included.

Link to this tip ↗
Getting startedFind Your Controls+

Open Menu → Settings → Volume Controls to adjust music and effects or turn Voice on or off. When guidance is muted, tap the voice button at the top of the screen to hear the current directions again. Menu → Guides opens the Field Guide; Bonus and Shop & Items group games and supplies. Gentle upbeat music varies by world and activity, with three arrangements across each world’s puzzle progress; warning cues become more urgent with 20 seconds or 5 moves left, and again at 7 seconds or 2 moves. On Android, the bottom menu shows your gold coins and lives throughout play. The puzzle header shows your level and score. Use Pause during a puzzle. Between levels, Menu → Worlds opens My World Map and Spin Quests.

Getting startedLives & Pause+

Lost lives refill over time. If you have banked lives, tap USE SAVED LIFE in the out-of-lives popup to claim one without visiting Inventory. On a failed level, then tap TRY AGAIN. Pause to take a break, restart or open sound settings. A failed level costs one life.

Puzzle stylesBloom Gather+

Tap 3+ same-colour gems touching up, down, left or right. Pairs do not clear. No swapping or diagonals. Groups can turn corners along shared edges. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesCross Gather+

Tap a gem: clear 2+ of its colour in straight lines up/down/left/right. No turns. The tapped gem counts toward the total. Each line stops at a different colour, gap or blocker. Lines cannot turn corners or go diagonally. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesDiagonal Gather+

Tap 2+ same-colour gems touching by an edge OR a corner. No swapping. The whole connected group clears, including diagonal connections. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesDiagonal Match+

Swap neighbours, including diagonally. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe between neighbouring gems, or tap one and then the other. You may swap diagonally, but a diagonal line does not count as a match.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesGather+

Tap 2+ same-colour gems touching up, down, left or right. No swapping or diagonals. Tap any gem in the connected group to clear it. Groups can turn corners along shared edges. After gems fall, new connections involving gems already on the board can clear automatically without another move. Groups made entirely of new refill gems wait for your next tap. Groups of 5–6 make a row power, 7–8 a bomb, and 9+ a rainbow. Tap a power to activate it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesMatch 3+

Swap up, down, left or right. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe between neighbouring gems, or tap one and then the other. Diagonal swaps and diagonal matches do not count. A swap that makes no match moves back.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesRising Tides+

Swap to match 3+ in a row or column. Pieces rise from below. Swap neighbouring pieces up, down, left or right. On delivery levels, clear ABOVE the pictured relic or charm to lift it to a TOP exit arrow.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesRiver Rows+

Shift a row one space left or right. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe left or right, or tap two horizontal neighbours. The connected row wraps at its ends; gaps and blockers stop the segment. Tap a power to fire it, or shift its row to fire it. A shift with no match or power moves back.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesStar Hop+

Tap a gem, then a gold-ringed gem to swap them. Line up 3 of one colour in a row or column. The two gems swap places in an L: two spaces across and one up or down, or two up or down and one across. Either end can complete the line. A hop with a power gem activates it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle stylesWind Columns+

Shift a column one space up or down. Make a row or column of 3+ matching gems. Swipe up or down, or tap two vertical neighbours. The connected column wraps at its ends; gaps and blockers stop the segment. Tap a power to fire it, or shift its column to fire it. A shift with no match or power moves back.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesBreak The Ice+

Look for the bright blue crystal frame and frosted corners. Match ON the tile or hit it with powers. Pale dots on the dark bottom badge show remaining layers.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesCombine Powers+

In swap styles, swap two neighboring power gems for a stronger combination. In shifting styles, every existing power in the shifted segment fires at its new position. Their clear areas work together.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesCrack The Stones+

Stone blocks stop swaps and falling gems. Adjacent matches break one layer; a direct power or hammer hit breaks two. The dots show remaining layers.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesCut The Vines+

Look for the frame of twisting copper roots and plum leaves. Match NEXT TO it. Pale dots on the dark bottom badge show remaining layers; gems can move through.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesDeliver Story Relics+

Delivery pieces include realm relics and character charms. Guide the pictured piece to an exit. Normally, clear pieces below it to reach the bottom gold arrows. In Rising Tides, clear ABOVE the relic to lift it to the TOP arrows. Relics cannot be blasted away.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesFree Reshuffles+

No legal matches, or the same small tap group keeps being your only option? Pip warns you before a free shuffle. Your goals stay in place, no booster is spent, and the clock pauses during the warning.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesLight The Story Route+

Match on beacon 1, then 2 and onward. Only the glowing numbered tile receives light; power hits work too.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesMake A Bomb+

Join a row of 3 and a column of 3 at one shared gem: 5 gems forming a T or L. This creates a bomb. In Gather styles, clear a group of 7 or 8 to create one. Swap or match a bomb to explode. In Gather and shifting styles, tap it to explode.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesNo-Match Penalties+

Hard, Extreme and Legendary: a swap or line shift without a match or activated power, or a group below the required size, costs 1 move or 3 seconds. Blocked swaps and selection taps cost nothing. Lower tiers have no penalty.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesOpen The Crates+

Look for the wooden crate with gold corners. Match beside it or hit it with powers. Pale dots on the dark bottom badge show how many hits remain.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesRainbow Power+

In swap and shifting styles, match five in a straight line. In Gather styles, clear a group of 9 or more. Swap the rainbow gem with a color to clear that color. In Gather or shifting styles, tapping it clears its own gem color.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesRow & Column Powers+

In swap and shifting styles, match four in a row to make a power gem. In Gather styles, clear a group of 5 or 6 to make a row power. Swap it with a neighbor to clear its row or column. In Gather or line-shifting styles, tap it; shifting a powered line also fires it.

Link to this tip ↗
Puzzle rulesSet Off Powers+

Create powers with 4 in a line, 5 in a line, or 5 in a T or L. Gather styles instead use groups of 5–6 for row powers, 7–8 for bombs, or 9+ for rainbows. Then swap or match them to fire; in Gather or shifting styles, tap a power. Shifting its line also activates it. Making one alone does not count as firing it.

Bonus gamesBonus Scatters+

Count Bonus symbols anywhere in the 15 full cells. Exactly two return your bet once; three unlock 2 free spins at 1× coins; four unlock 3 at 2×; five or more unlock 6 at 3×. Every 3+ trigger also opens Pip’s Firefly Flight. Free spins can retrigger both rewards. Flight winnings do not use the free-spin multiplier. See FIREFLY FLIGHT below for controls, health and earned prizes. Older unfinished lantern and chest bonuses keep their original rules.

Bonus gamesDaily Discovery+

Open Discovery beside Wheel and Slots. Compare both pictures and tap a changed detail in either one. Pinch to zoom and drag to inspect. Find eight changes before your misses run out. Your first hint in each round is free; later hints cost 50 coins. A pink ring marks the detail; viewing an existing hint again is free. The play count includes this realm’s free or unfinished scene plus saved Discovery tokens. The first three completed scenes across all realms each award 100 coins and one hint daily; further scenes are practice. Prizes reset at local midnight. New rounds use visible object changes: a missing piece, a different shape or a changed count. Look for changes in painted colours, shapes and details. The three most recent compositions are avoided. The previous five clue sets are avoided. Unfinished rounds keep your clues, hints and progress, including after midnight. Out of tries? One optional ad restores one mistake per round and keeps your finds. Watch one ad to start a fresh scene, or use a replay token. A Discovery replay token starts a fresh round after a finished or failed scene with one free hint and an extra completion reward; use the token replay button on the Discovery result panel, or open Inventory → Tokens.

Bonus gamesDaily Pairs+

Open Pairs beside Wheel, Slots and Discovery, or in Menu → Bonus. Find ten matching pairs among 20 cards. New rounds rotate through all 50 friends; saved rounds keep their faces. Revealing two cards counts as one move. Finish in 10–12 moves for one rainbow booster, 13–20 for one hammer, 21–30 for 150 coins, or 31+ for 75 coins. Unfinished six-pair rounds keep their original reward thresholds. No entry fee, timer or life cost. Cards flip as you reveal them; matching pairs sparkle. Mismatches stay visible briefly. Cards and moves save when you leave. One completed round per local day; unfinished rounds resume after midnight and use the completion day's allowance. Rewards save automatically; the last pair celebrates before the prize panel opens. Close the panel to return. Badges count the free or unfinished round plus saved tokens. Watch one ad on the completed board for another round, or buy a Pairs token in Coin Shop → Tokens or Market Place → Token Packs and use it directly in Pairs or Inventory → Tokens after completing today’s round.

Bonus gamesFirefly Flight+

Guide Pip with touch, drag, or arrow keys. Rescue five fireflies in each clearing, then enter its portal. Each clearing gets harder: 2 gusts, then 3 faster gusts and 2 thorn patches, then 4 fast crossing gusts and 4 thorn patches. Steer around the purple thorns and moving gusts. A one-second safe entry lets you orient yourself in each clearing. Start with 100% bonus health. Each gust or thorn hit removes 25%; a brief recovery shield prevents repeated damage from the same contact. At 0%, the flight ends and you keep only what you earned. Each rescue earns 1/15 of the coin treasure, rounded down cumulatively. Any available non-rainbow booster unlocks at 10 rescues; an available rainbow unlocks at 15. The bottom panel shows earned winnings, starting at zero. Health and rescues are saved; reopening does not heal Pip. Bonus health is separate from puzzle lives. Your regular free spins wait for your return.
